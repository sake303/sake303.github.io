About this evaluation

This application is a private local evaluation of the Open Wearables Oura integration. The operator uses an authorized Oura account to observe one completed sleep session through the scheduled pull, API, and Sleep screen. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts or access to other users.

Authorization

The account holder chooses whether to authorize access to Oura Personal and Daily data. Access may be revoked through Oura's connected-app controls. Revocation stops future authorized access; the Privacy Notice explains how to request deletion of data already stored in the local environment.

Limits

This evaluation does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is limited to observing data delivery in a local Open Wearables installation. Oura's own terms separately govern the Oura service.
