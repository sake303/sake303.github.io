# My daily rhythm and the spaces I like

## Rhythm

I outline my day in my calendar and adjust it to fit my schedule and how I'm feeling.

| Time | What I do |
| --- | --- |
| 00:00–06:00 | Sleep · at least 6 hours |
| 07:00–08:00 | Exercise · running · cycling · basketball |
| Morning | Start with my top priority |
| After that | Complete one cycle of my loop |
| Before wrapping up the day | Evidence / Review: look back at the day's evidence and results |
