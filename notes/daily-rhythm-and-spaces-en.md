# My daily rhythm and the spaces I like

## Daily rhythm

I usually start by blocking out the kind of day I want in my calendar. I set aside seven hours for sleep and make time for exercise. In the morning, I focus on the one thing that matters most. After lunch, I set aside two blocks for work, with room in between for meals, getting around, and walks.

My focus time is usually in 90-minute blocks. If I have appointments or time in the field, I combine or move the blocks around and adjust for how I'm feeling. In the evening, I take a quick look back at the day, write down what's left to do, and try to leave the rest of the evening for myself.

## Spaces and cafes

Home is where I rest and recover. When I want to focus on making something, I tend to go out. Working at a cafe, sorting through my thoughts as I move between places, and heading somewhere to exercise is a rhythm that suits me.

I'd like to keep living this way in other cities, too. I like places where I can work and exercise, meet people and talk, and enjoy sports. I'm interested in spending time in a place and finding my own daily rhythm there.
