# My daily rhythm and the spaces I like

## Why I want to live this way

I want to spend as much time as I can in the field, meeting people and learning by observing real problems. That includes building trust and having people try what I make, as well as working out together and taking part in communities.

When I'm on my own, I want to learn about the fundamentals and connect what I learn to my experiences. I want time to read philosophy, mathematics, and science, and to write and speak in English.

Even if AI becomes more capable than humans, I think we still have reasons to study. Learning gives us a chance to understand things for ourselves, gain insight, ask new questions, and develop our thinking through conversations with others. Getting an answer doesn't mean the purpose of studying has been fulfilled.

I want to delegate research, problem definition, design, building, and improvement broadly to AI. I look at the results and the reasoning, ask why a choice was made, check how useful it is in practice, and change direction when needed. I want to get as much done as possible with a small team, and use the time that opens up to learn and meet people.

## Rhythm

I want a life where I learn through experience in the field, study and reflect when I'm on my own, and use AI to get things done while keeping my body and daily rhythm in shape. I outline my day in my calendar and adjust it to fit my schedule and how I'm feeling.

| Part of the day | What I aim for |
| --- | --- |
| Sleep | At least 6 hours, and more when I need it |
| Exercise | About an hour a day · running · cycling · basketball |
| In the field | Meet people, observe real problems, see how useful a product is, and take part in sport and communities |
| On my own | Learn about the fundamentals, connect them to my experiences, and write and speak in English |
| Working with AI | Delegate research through building and improvement, question the results and reasoning, and check their usefulness in practice |
| Downtime | Leave room for rest |

## A source behind this thought

[A declaration by Fields Medalists](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/09/11/a-severe-misalignment-of-ai-in-mathematics/) describes solving mathematical problems as a means toward conceptual understanding and insight. The connection to studying more broadly and to the life I want is my own.

I plan to keep adding questions and notes that connect to my experiences in [this collection](fundamentals-en.md).
