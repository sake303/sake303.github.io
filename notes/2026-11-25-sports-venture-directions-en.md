| Sleep and jet lag | Wearable sleep data, scheduling data, APIs |

**APIs**

- **DLY** uses measurements and training records to give staff the information they need to review an athlete's return to play in the software their team already uses.
- **FRAME** uses game footage and player stats to help sports apps explain the plays fans ask about.
- **PHASE** uses actual sleep records and the remaining travel schedule to give existing apps practical options for sleep and light exposure.

**Dataset**

- **VECTOR** is a motion dataset I'm exploring for researchers and developers. It would turn footage and sensor readings from sports settings into data they can use to train and evaluate models.
