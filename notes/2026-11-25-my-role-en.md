# My role

In team sports, I like to [do my job and create chances for my teammates](2026-11-25-team-play-en.md). On defense, I cover my assignment. On offense, I look for ways to put teammates in a position to do what they do best.

The [work I want to get better at myself](founder-time-work-benchmark-en.md) is seeing how people work firsthand and deciding which problems to tackle and who to work with.

I'm building [infrastructure that partners can share](connected-ecosystem-business-rationale-en.md#how-i-want-to-build-a-partner-ecosystem). I take a cue from [Terra API](https://tryterra.co/) and design each integration so several partners can use it. As new integrations help existing partners, I think maintaining those connections and growing the partner ecosystem are what make the business harder to copy.
