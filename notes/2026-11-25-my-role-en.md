# My role

In team sports, I like to [do my job and help my teammates get good opportunities](2026-11-25-team-play-en.md). On defense, I cover my assignment. On offense, I look for ways to put teammates in a position to do what they do best.

I want to get better at seeing how people work firsthand and deciding which problems to tackle and who to work with. I take a cue from [Clay co-founder Varun Anand](founder-time-work-benchmark-en.md), who watched customers use Clay to see where they got stuck.

I want to build [infrastructure that partners can share](connected-ecosystem-business-rationale-en.md#how-i-want-to-build-a-partner-ecosystem). With a setup like [Terra API](https://tryterra.co/), several partners can use the same integration. Data providers can reach more customers, and product teams have fewer integrations to build and maintain. If a new integration also helps existing partners, the network becomes harder to replace.
