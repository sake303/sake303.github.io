In team sports, I like to [do my job and create chances for my teammates](2026-11-25-team-play-en.md). On defense, I cover my assignment. On offense, I look for ways to put teammates in a position to do what they do best.

I approach business the same way. [Doing my part](founder-time-work-benchmark-en.md) starts with listening to people and seeing firsthand how they work. I want to get better at using what I learn there to [build infrastructure that connects partners](connected-ecosystem-business-rationale-en.md#how-i-want-to-build-a-partner-ecosystem). I take a cue from [Terra API](https://tryterra.co/) and design each integration so several partners can use it. As new integrations help existing partners, I think maintaining those connections and growing the partner ecosystem are what make the business harder to copy.
